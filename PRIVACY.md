# Unbrace privacy policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Unbrace is a Chrome extension that formats JSON documents in your browser.

**Unbrace does not collect, store, or transmit any data.** When you open a page, it checks locally whether the page is a JSON document and, if so, renders it as a formatted tree inside that same tab. The page contents never leave your browser, and Unbrace makes no network requests of its own.

Unbrace uses no analytics, cookies, accounts, or third-party services, and does not sell or share any information.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
